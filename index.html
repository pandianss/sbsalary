Operating instructions

Rules every branch must follow

Aligned with the IOB website Salary Account page (updated 19.10.2026), which takes precedence over the 2023 circulars where they differ.

1

Salary mandate is required

A mandate from the employer for crediting salary into the IOB Salary Account is required at opening.

2

PAI cover needs salary credit

Gold, Diamond and Platinum: cover is activated when salary is credited and continues while regular credit continues. IOB Premium: cover and other insurance benefits are activated only after salary credit for 3 consecutive months, then regular credits, and cease on retirement, switch, or 2 months of non-credit.

3

Retirement / switch / no credit for 2 months

On retirement, switching organisation, or non-credit of salary for two consecutive months, the account migrates to regular SB Public. The customer maintains the SB Public minimum balance, all free facilities and concessions are withdrawn, and the branch takes recovery measures where necessary.

4

No flexi (sweep) deposit

Flexi deposit is not available in Gold, Diamond or Platinum. Existing FFDs created under old salary schemes run off until maturity.

5

Portability after one year

Moving between Gold, Diamond and Platinum is allowed only after one year from account opening. Existing customers keep the same account number.

6

Debit card variants

Customers may choose any variant, but free issuance/AMC applies only to the approved variant or lower. A higher variant is charged unless the required balance is maintained.

7

Loan concessions

Concession must never push the rate below the benchmark rate. Multiple concessions are not allowed. Processing-fee concession applies only to listed retail schemes.

8

Card benefits are network-driven

Lounge access and card-linked PAI come from NPCI / VISA / RuPay and may change. RuPay insurance needs a POS/Ecom transaction once in 30 days. Refer customers to the provider's website.

9

Overdraft conditions

Only in an emergency; one salary credit is mandatory; max 2 occasions in a calendar year.

10

Explain before opening

Branch heads must sensitise staff and ensure scheme details, including the retirement/non-credit rule and Gold charges, are explained to customers before the account is opened.

11

IOB Premium: confirmed employees only

Net monthly salary credit above ₹1.50 lakh (average of last 3 months), confirmed in service. Collect salary slip and appointment letter along with standard documents. Excludes armed forces and police for the PAI cover.

Circular Dep/15/2025-26 (18.09.2025): IOB Premium is operational from 18.09.2025. The revised Gold/Diamond/Platinum features are effective 01.11.2025, after one month's notice to existing customers; branches should display them on notice boards. Account journey

From canvassing to conversion

A simple checklist for branches handling a salary account from first conversation to year-end.

Identify

Confirm employer type, age 18–65 and avg. net salary of last 3 months.

Match variant

Gold, Diamond or Platinum by salary band. Explain the lifestyle perks and cards.

Open & mandate

Obtain the employer salary mandate. Offer family accounts and alias name.

Activate

Salary credit activates PAI cover. Guide customer on card, OD and digital banking.

Review yearly

After 1 year, offer upgrade. If salary stops for 2 months, migrate to SB Public.

Staff FAQ

Questions branches ask

Customer's net salary is ₹30,000 — which variant?

Diamond (above ₹25,000 up to ₹75,000). The tier is based on the average net salary of the last 3 months. Up to ₹25,000 is Gold; above ₹75,000 up to ₹1,50,000 is Platinum.

Who qualifies for IOB Premium (SBCSP)?

Confirmed employees of Government organisations, PSUs, MNCs, reputed corporates and institutions (including educational institutions, hospitals, hotels, transport corporations) with an average net monthly salary credit above ₹1.50 lakh over the last 3 months, aged 18–65. Collect the salary slip and appointment letter with the usual documents.

Is IOB Premium the same as the old SB Premium / SBPRM?

No. SBPRM (IOB Corp Premium) was merged into Gold in 2023. IOB Premium (SBCSP) is a new scheme launched 18.09.2025 with ₹100 lakh accident cover, hospital cash, cyber insurance and a 4× salary overdraft.

Is there a minimum balance?

No. Minimum balance is Nil in all three variants. After migration to SB Public, that scheme's minimum balance applies.

What happens if salary stops coming?

If salary is not credited for 2 consecutive months, or on retirement or switching organisations, the account migrates to regular SB Public. The customer must maintain the SB Public minimum balance, all free facilities and concessions are withdrawn, and the branch takes recovery measures where necessary.

Can a customer have flexi (sweep-in/out) deposit?

No. Flexi deposit is not available in the three salary variants. Customers who want it can be guided to a scheme where it is available. Existing FFDs run off until maturity.

Does the free accident cover apply to everyone?

Yes (armed forces and police are excluded for Premium). Cover is ₹20 L / ₹30 L / ₹40 L (Death + PPD & TPD) with the account. Debit-card insurance via NPCI is separate: ₹2 L (Gold, RuPay Platinum) or ₹10 L (Diamond/Platinum, RuPay Select), subject to a POS/Ecom transaction once in 30 days.

Who gets "My Account My Name"?

IOB Salary Account and SB HNI customers. After opening, the customer logs in on the bank's website, enters the 15-digit account number, validates with an OTP on the registered mobile, then chooses a unique alias.

Can concessions be combined?

No. Multiple concessions are not allowed, and a concession must never bring the retail loan rate below the benchmark rate. Rate concessions apply to Platinum only.

Which loans get a processing-fee concession?

Home loans, plus retail schemes CCROY, DLCLN, CCCLN, RPLHI, RPLOT, RROYL, RPUSH and CCLAP/RHNIL.
